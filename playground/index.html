The gate is the product

Agents don't obey "be careful." They obey the constraints they were handed — or they don't execute at all. This ring is the entire enforcement mechanism of this implementation: an amount arrives, the profile’s rules decide, and the action is or isn't allowed to exist. Nothing else happens. 02

Authority narrows — by construction

AR o o t of trust is human

Everything starts with a signed delegation from a human to an agent — scoped to one action type, one system, a hard cap, and 30 minutes. No evergreen authority. No ambient permissions.

BNarrowing is mandatory, not aspirational

If the agent re-delegates — to a creative writer, an analyst, an incident responder — every constraint visibly shrinks. Permissions intersect. Amount caps drop. Budgets draw down. A wider child grant is rejected at creation, before it can exist.

CEnforcement lives at the point of action

Not at login. Not at session start. The gate re-checks on every single call — cap, budget, threshold, revocation. The agent doesn't ask for permission; the gate decides whether the action exists.

DRevocation bites in-flight

Revoke the leaf delegation and the next tool call is denied — with a receipt. No rotation ceremony, no waiting for token expiry, no Am I allowed to do X asking the IdP.

04

The receipt is the product

The outcome of any incident is a piece of paper: which agent, under whose authority, with what credential, what action, under what approval. This implementation produces that paper as a matter of course — signed, chained, hash-verified, and re-derivable by anyone holding the inputs.

SignedES256 — tamper-evident by construction
Chainedeach receipt hash-links the previous; one altered receipt breaks the seal
Content-addressedevery receipt names the exact validation profile that decided it
